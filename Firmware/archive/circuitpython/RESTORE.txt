This is the CircuitPython v8 wand program, kept so it can be put back.

1. Hold BOOTSEL while plugging the wand in. A drive called RP2350 appears.
2. Copy circuitpython-pico2-10.3.1.uf2 onto that drive and wait for it to reboot.
3. A drive called CIRCUITPY appears. Copy everything in device/ onto it, and copy code.py last.
4. Leave the wand still for a second. The serial line is POV> ready (v8).
